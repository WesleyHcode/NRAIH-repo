Instructions

Searching

1. In the "Search" menu, you will see a textbox for your search.

2. Click "Enter" once you've typed out your prompt.

3. Under "AI Response," you can see some of the most relevant news stories that were found.

4. Each story includes a title, date, AI generated bias and perspective report, and a link to the original article.

Lookup

1. In the "Lookup" menu, you will see a textbox for your lookup search.

2. Click "Enter" once you've typed out your prompt.

3. Under "AI Response," you can see if the AI found your story, and if it has generated a report. This may not always for due to sourcing restraints.

How it Works

Process

When the user searches, we can fetch the most relevant and popular news stories relating to that prompt using NewsAPI. We can then send this as a prompt to Groq's AI API, instructing it to generate us a report in JSON, where the AI decides its perspective, bias, and important context that might have been left out. Taking the JSON report, we can feature it as a full report of a news story.
